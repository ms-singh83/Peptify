# Peptify — Launch & Growth Plan (first 30 days)

PeptIQ's revenue comes from distribution (SEO, TikTok/IG, influencers, ASO), not features. Start during build week.

## During build week (30 min/day)

- Create TikTok, Instagram, X accounts @peptifyapp. Post a daily "building a peptide app in 7 days" clip (screen recording + voice).
- Join communities and **listen first**: r/Peptides, r/Semaglutide, r/tirzepatide, biohacking Discords. Note the exact questions people ask. Those become content + features.
- Landing page (one page): headline, 3 screenshots, store badges, email capture for Android beta testers (doubles as your 12 closed testers).

## Launch week

- Content pillars (3 posts/day across TikTok/Reels/Shorts):
  1. **Calculator demos:** "How many units is 250 mcg from a 5 mg vial?" (highest search intent).
  2. **Organization:** "How I track 3 peptides without a spreadsheet".
  3. **Education:** neutral half-life/storage explainers with the disclaimer.
- Reply helpfully in communities. Only mention the app when it directly answers the question.
- Ask the first 20 happy users for a review (`expo-store-review` prompt after the 5th logged dose).

## Days 8–30

- **SEO:** 10 pages on the landing site: "peptide reconstitution calculator" (a free web calculator — biggest traffic driver), "how to rotate injection sites", one page per popular peptide (storage + half-life).
- **Micro-influencers** (5–50k followers, fitness/GLP-1 journeys): free Pro + 30% affiliate via RevenueCat/Promo codes.
- **ASO:** after 2 weeks, test icon + first screenshot; track keyword ranks.
- **Paid** (only after trial→paid ≥ 10%): small Meta/TikTok test, $20/day, calculator-demo creative. Note: platforms restrict injectable/peptide ads — keep creatives about tracking.

## Weekly metrics (RevenueCat + store consoles)

Installs · onboarding completion · trial starts · trial→paid % · MRR · D1/D7 retention · avg doses logged/user/week.
