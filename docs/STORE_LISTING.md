## Full Description ≤4000 chars (same for iOS and Play)

Peptify is the simple, private way to stay consistent with your peptide protocol.

**Never miss a dose.** Build your protocol (daily, specific weekdays, every few days, or on/off cycles) and get reminders at the right time. One tap marks a dose taken or skipped.

**Log every detail.** Record injection site, actual dose, and notes. Peptify suggests your least-recently-used site so rotation is easy to keep track of.

**Vial math in seconds.** The reconstitution calculator turns vial size, water added and your target dose into the exact syringe units to draw. Visual syringe reference included.

**Know what's left.** Track your vials and watch remaining amounts drop with every logged dose. Get alerts when stock runs low or expiry approaches.

**Learn as you go.** A clear, referenced library of 25 peptides: what they are, what has been studied in research, half-life and storage guidance.

**Private by design.** Your data stays on your phone. No account, no analytics, no syncing to servers.

### Peptify Pro

Peptify Pro unlocks unlimited protocols, reminders for every protocol, vial inventory tracking, full dose history, the complete 25-peptide library and CSV export for your records.

**Pricing:** Peptify Pro offers monthly and yearly subscription options. A 3-day free trial is available for annual plans.

**Auto-renewal:** Your subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of your billing period. You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renewal in your device account settings.

---

**Disclaimer:** Peptify is a personal tracking and education tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not recommend any substance or dose. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before starting, changing or stopping any protocol.
