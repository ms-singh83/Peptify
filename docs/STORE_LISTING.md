## Full description

Peptify is the simple, private way to stay consistent with your peptide protocol.

**Never miss a dose.** Build your protocol (daily, specific weekdays, every few days, or on/off cycles) and get reminders at the right time.

**Log in one tap.** Mark doses taken or skipped, record the injection site, and let Peptify suggest your next site so you rotate properly.

**Vial math in seconds.** The reconstitution calculator turns vial size, water added and your dose into the exact syringe units to draw.

**Know what's left.** Track vials, see remaining amount drop with every dose, and get low-stock and expiry alerts.

**Learn as you go.** A clear, referenced library of 25 peptides: what they are, what has been studied, half-life and storage.

**Private by design.** Your data stays on your phone. No account needed.

Peptify Pro unlocks unlimited protocols, reminders for every protocol, vial inventory, full history, the complete library and CSV export.

Peptify is for tracking and education only. It does not provide medical advice or recommend any substance or dose. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional.
