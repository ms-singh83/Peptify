# Peptify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_ · Operated by [COMPANY / YOUR NAME] · Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

**Summary:** Your tracking data stays on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't sell data, and we don't run ads.

## Data stored on your device only
Protocols, doses, injection sites, vials, notes and settings you enter are saved locally on your phone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

## Data processed by third parties
- **Purchases (RevenueCat, Apple, Google):** when you subscribe, the app store and RevenueCat process your purchase and an anonymous app user ID to unlock Pro. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
- **Notifications:** reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent to our servers.

## Data we do not collect
No name, email, location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifiers.

## Children
Peptify is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Your rights
Because your data is on your device, you control it fully: export it (CSV, Pro) or delete it by removing the app. For purchase data, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Changes
We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
