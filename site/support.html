Frequently Asked Questions
Where is my data stored?

All your data—protocols, doses, injection sites, vials, and notes—is stored locally on your phone in a private database. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, the data is deleted. Your data is never sent to our servers or shared with anyone.

How do I restore my Peptify Pro purchase?

Open Peptify and go to Settings → Restore Purchases. This will verify your subscription with Apple or Google. If you recently purchased or switched devices, restoring will unlock Pro features without being charged again.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone (App Store):

  • Open the Settings app
  • Tap your name at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Tap Peptify
  • Tap Cancel Subscription

On Android (Google Play):

  • Open the Google Play app
  • Tap your profile icon
  • Tap Payments and subscriptions
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Tap Peptify
  • Tap Cancel subscription
Why are my reminders not arriving?

Check these settings on your phone:

  • Notification permission: Make sure Peptify can send notifications. Go to Settings → Apps → Peptify → Notifications and turn on "Allow notifications".
  • Focus or Do Not Disturb: If you have Focus or Do Not Disturb turned on, it may silence Peptify notifications. Check your Focus settings or turn off Do Not Disturb during your dose times.
  • Battery optimization (Android only): Some Android phones restrict background apps to save battery. Go to Settings → Battery → Battery Optimization and set Peptify to "Not optimized" to allow reminders to work.
How do I delete my data?

All your data is stored only on your phone. To delete it, simply uninstall Peptify. The app and all associated data (protocols, doses, notes, settings) will be removed from your device. If you later reinstall the app, you'll start fresh with no previous data.

Is Peptify medical advice?

No. Peptify is a personal tracking and education tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and does not recommend any substance or dose. The app describes what has been studied in research and performs calculations based on numbers you enter. You are responsible for your own decisions. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before starting, changing or stopping any protocol.
